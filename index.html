About the Artist

Manesong Kenyem Tchoumen

Manesong Kenyem Tchoumen holds a BFA degree from the Institute of Fine Arts Nkongsamba.

Inspired by the beauty of nature, its relationship with man and God, he seeks to create artworks that invite people to recognize the Glory of God in nature and to align themselves as well to glorify God in their lives.

“Beauty can point beyond itself.”
